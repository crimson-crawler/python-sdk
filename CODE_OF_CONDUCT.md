# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We welcome technical disagreement and expect participants to treat one another with respect. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and unauthorized security testing are not acceptable.

Examples of acceptable participation include giving specific technical feedback, acknowledging mistakes, respecting boundaries, and keeping discussions focused on the project.

## Scope

This policy applies to this repository and to project-related communication in other public or private spaces when a person is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed in the repository's security policy. Do not open a public issue containing private information. Maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy and safety of those involved, and take action they consider appropriate, including editing or removing contributions and temporarily or permanently restricting participation.

Maintainers must apply this policy fairly and may recuse themselves when they have a conflict of interest.
